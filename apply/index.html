Accepting talent applications for our founding roster.

If you work in anime, cosplay, manga, comics, or games, you can put your name forward. This is an expression of interest. It is not a representation agreement, and it does not promise a job.

No size of following is required. A team or a studio can apply under one name. Voice, music, esports, and the rest of the category map are open too.

Proposed support

These are plans for after licensing and contracts. They are not services Anistar can deliver today.

A place in your category
Anistar would present your portfolio in the category that fits the work, so a reader sees a cosplayer, a mangaka, a voice actor, or a team.
Introductions
Introductions to other creators in related categories, when both people want the conversation.
Interviews and short features
Interviews and short features about the work, as content Anistar plans to make. A feature is not guaranteed.
Cross-market work, after licensing
Brand and convention collaborations, and representation, for creators who want work to travel among North America, South America, Japan, Europe, and the Middle East. An application does not sign you, book you, set a fee, get you a visa, or open a door to a named studio.

Prepare your application

Prepare it, then copy it. Nothing is sent. This page does not store it. Anistar has no monitored inbox and no privacy notice yet, so nothing here is reviewed and nothing is answered. A live application needs both of those before it can be delivered.

Ko Mori, Henry Finn, and the plan

Co-founder

Ko Mori

Co-founder of Anistar. Founder and CEO of ELEVEN ARTS.

Ko Mori is a co-founder of Anistar and the founder and CEO of ELEVEN ARTS in Los Angeles. ELEVEN ARTS is a separate company. It releases Japanese anime for audiences in North America. That record is why Anistar's plan is connecting talent across East and West.

The intended focus is representation in North America, South America, Japan, Europe, and the Middle East. Those regions are a plan. Anistar has no offices there, holds no local licenses, and does not arrange visas or cross-border jobs.
