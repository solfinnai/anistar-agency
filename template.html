Connecting talent across East and West

The aim is to represent creators who want their work to travel. The intended focus is North America, South America, Japan, Europe, and the Middle East.

The founding roster starts with applications from anime, cosplay, manga, comics, and games, and from the wider map: voice, music, esports teams, and game studios.

Co-founders: Ko Mori and Henry Finn.
