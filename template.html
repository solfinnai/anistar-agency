The agency

Anistar is a talent agency for anime, cosplay, and creator talent, and for manga, comics, and games.

Conventions, publishers, and brands hire through the agency. Artists, performers, and teams ask the agency to represent them. Esports teams and indie game studios sit on the roster as talent. A team is booked the same way a cosplayer or an illustrator is booked.

The agency is co-founded by Henry Finn and Ko Mori.

Representation

Rates, agreements, and which job should come next. The work between the bookings.

Partnerships

A publisher or a brand wants a specific person for a campaign or a licensed piece. Anistar sits in the middle of that conversation.

Live appearances

A convention weekend, a panel, a signing, a premiere, or a stream.

Commissions

The piece itself. A drawing, a voice, a track, a script, a costume, or a stage.
