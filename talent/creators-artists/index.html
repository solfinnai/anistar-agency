SPECIALTIES

Anime-Style Illustrators

Character and key-visual art in anime aesthetics.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Key visual artist, fan-art illustrator, splash artist

Manga Artists (Mangaka)

Authors and artists of serialized manga.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Mangaka, one-shot artist, doujinshi creator

Manga Assistants

Background, tone and finishing support for manga production.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Background assistant, screentone artist, finisher

Western Comic Artists

Pencillers and artists for US/EU comics and graphic novels.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Penciller, cover artist, graphic novelist

Comic Inkers & Colorists

Finishing specialists for comics and manga.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Inker, flatter, colorist

Letterers & Typesetters

Lettering, SFX and typesetting for comics and localized manga.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Letterer, SFX artist, typesetter

Webtoon Creators

Vertical-scroll comic storytellers.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Webtoon author, line artist, webtoon colorist

Webcomic & Indie Comic Creators

Independent comic makers on web and print.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Webcomic author, zine maker, Kickstarter creator

Concept Artists

Visual development for anime, games and film.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Character concept artist, environment concept artist

Character Designers

Original and adaptation character design.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Character designer, costume designer (2D), mascot designer

Cover & Book Illustrators

Covers and interior art for light novels and books.

EXAMPLE ROLES: LN illustrator, cover artist, children's book illustrator

Trading Card & Collectible Artists

Art for TCGs, gacha cards and collectibles.

EXAMPLE ROLES: TCG illustrator, gacha artist, sticker artist

Pixel Artists

Retro and indie pixel art for games and media.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Sprite artist, pixel animator, tileset artist

3D Character Artists & Sculptors

3D models, figures and digital sculpts.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Figure sculptor, 3D modeler, garage-kit maker

Merch & Product Designers

Designers of apparel, plush, acrylics and goods.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Merch designer, plush designer, packaging designer

Tattoo & Street Artists

Anime-inspired tattoo, mural and graffiti artists.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Tattoo artist, muralist, live painter

NFT / Digital Collectible Artists

Web3 and digital-collectible art creators.

EXAMPLE ROLES: PFP artist, generative artist

Art Streamers & Speedpainters

Artists who create live for audiences.

EXAMPLE ROLES: Live drawing streamer, speedpainter, artist alley performer
