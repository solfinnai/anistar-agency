  1. Key Animators

    Primary animation drawings for anime.

    Example roles. Genga artist, action animator, sakuga animator

  2. In-Between & Cleanup Animators

    Douga and cleanup for smooth motion.

    Example roles. In-betweener, cleanup artist

  3. Animation & Episode Directors

    Direction of series, episodes and storyboards.

    Example roles. Series director, episode director, unit director

  4. Storyboard Artists

    Visual planning of scenes for anime, games and film.

    Example roles. Storyboarder, e-conte artist, animatic editor

  5. Background Artists & Art Directors

    Environment painting and art direction.

    Example roles. Background painter, art director, color designer

  6. 3DCG & Compositing Artists

    CG animation, VFX and photography/compositing.

    Example roles. 3DCG animator, compositor, VFX artist

  7. Motion Graphics & Opening Designers

    OP/ED sequences, title design and motion.

    Example roles. Motion designer, OP/ED creator, lyric-video artist

  8. Live2D Riggers & Modelers

    Rigging for VTubers and games.

    Example roles. Live2D rigger, VTuber model artist, 3D VTuber modeler

  9. Animation Producers

    Production management for anime projects.

    Example roles. Animation producer, production assistant, line producer

  10. Independent Animators

    Indie shorts and YouTube animation creators.

    Example roles. Indie animator, animation YouTuber, AMV creator

  11. Editors & Post-Production

    Editing and finishing for anime, trailers and content.

    Example roles. Video editor, trailer editor, colorist (video)
