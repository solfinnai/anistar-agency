03 / HOW IT WORKS

Two clear paths.

For partners

  1. Build a brief. Add the specialties you need from the talent map.
  2. Share the essentials. Project type, timing and budget range in one short form.
  3. Get a shortlist. Anistar matches the brief with suitable, available talent.

For talent

  1. Apply. Tell us your field and share your strongest work.
  2. Review. We look at craft, consistency and fit with active briefs.
  3. Representation. Agreed terms, then bookings, deals and career planning.

    08 / FAQ

    Questions.

    Is the roster live?+

    Not yet. Anistar is mapping the fields it will represent before it publishes anyone. Every roster slot on this site is a placeholder, and people or teams will appear only after they have agreed to be listed.

    What kinds of talent does Anistar represent?+

    Anistar's scope covers 16 divisions and 135 specialties across anime, manga, comics, webtoons and gaming, from illustrators and voice actors to VTubers, esports teams and indie game studios.

    Do esports teams and game studios count as talent?+

    Yes. Esports teams and organizations and independent game studios are represented as talent on the roster, not treated as clients.

    How do I book talent or send a brief?+

    Add the specialties you need to a brief, then complete the short inquiry form. You will get a clean summary of your request to send or copy.

    How do I apply for representation?+

    Use the roster application below. Share your field, your strongest work and the links that show it. Applications open fully once the intake process is staffed.
