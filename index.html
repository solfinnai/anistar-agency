The company

The work below is planned. Representation and booking are not offered on this site.

Readers can study the category map and prepare a planning note. Adults who work in these fields can prepare a talent interest note. Esports teams and game studios are talent categories, with cosplayers, illustrators, and voice actors.

Co-founders: Henry Finn and Ko Mori.

Planned work

Representation
Agreements with talent, once the agency can offer them. Unavailable now.
Partnerships
A future conversation between a category and a project. Anistar does not arrange partnerships on this site.
Live appearances
Conventions, panels, signings, premieres, and streams are categories on the map. Appearances cannot be booked here.
Commissions
Drawings, voice, music, scripts, costumes, and stage work are categories on the map. Commissions cannot be placed here.
