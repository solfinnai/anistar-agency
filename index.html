01 / ABOUT

The creative work deserves thoughtful representation.

Anistar is taking shape around the people who make and share fandom culture. The agency's planned scope reaches from illustration, animation and voice performance to streaming, esports and independent game development.

We are mapping the fields we intend to serve before publishing a roster. Every listed specialty describes an area of focus, not an available person or team.

02 / OUR FOCUS

Support across the creative career.

We are developing a model for representation that fits the range of work in anime, comics and gaming.
